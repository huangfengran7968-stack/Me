Quick start guide for your smart ring. Connect, wear, and begin your digital companionship journey.

1

Download the DeepSire App

Download the DeepSire app from the options below, or search "DeepSire" in your app store. Grant Bluetooth permissions when prompted to enable ring pairing.

Tip: Before first use, ensure your phone has network access and allow the app to use Bluetooth permissions for searching and connecting the Touch Ring.

2

Wear & Wake the Touch Ring

Ensure the ring is sufficiently charged. Wear it on your finger with the inner sensor area naturally touching your skin. Choose a snug but non-restrictive fit — not too loose or too tight — for stable data measurement.

Tip: Keep your hand still during measurement and avoid vigorous movement to improve data stability.

Scenario A: Standalone Ring Use

For users who only use the Touch Ring and want to view status data or perform single measurements via the DeepSire app.

3A

Connect in the App

Open the DeepSire app, sign in or create an account, then tap "Add Device" and select "Touch Ring". Follow the prompts to enable Bluetooth. The system will automatically search for nearby Touch Ring devices. Select your ring from the list to complete pairing. Once connected, the ring will appear on your device dashboard.

Tip: If no devices are found, ensure the ring is charged, Bluetooth is enabled, and bring the ring closer to your phone before searching again.

4A

View Health Data

After connection, tap the Touch Ring device card to enter the data page. View daily status data collected by the ring, including heart rate, sleep patterns, stress levels, and more. Displayed content varies by ring version and app version.

Note: Data is for daily reference only and is not intended for medical diagnosis, treatment, or health assessment.

5A

Take a Single Measurement

In the health data page, select the measurement type you want (e.g., heart rate, stress). Tap the corresponding item, follow the prompts to keep your hand still, and wait for the measurement to complete. Results will appear on the app page.

Tip: Keep the ring stable during measurement. Avoid frequent finger movement to prevent inaccurate results.

Scenario B: Link with Sight Device

For users who already have a DeepSire Sight device and want to link the Touch Ring for interactive companionship.

3B

Connect via Sight Interface

Open the DeepSire app and enter the interface of your bound Sight device. Tap "Add Device" or "Link Touch Ring". Follow the prompts to enable Bluetooth. The system will search for nearby Touch Ring devices. Bring the ring close to both your phone and Sight device, then wait for it to appear in the list.

4B

Pair the Touch Ring

Select your Touch Ring from the search results and tap to pair. Once paired, the ring will be linked to your Sight device account. Status data collected by the ring can now be used for interactive responses from your digital life on Sight.

Tip: If you have multiple rings, confirm the correct one by its device name or number to avoid binding to the wrong device.

5B

Measure & Interact with Sight

After pairing, wear the ring properly and select a measurement item in the app. Follow the prompts to stay still during measurement. Once complete, data can be viewed in the app. If Sight linking is enabled, your digital companion can respond based on your status — offering gentle reminders, caring interactions, or companionship feedback.

Example: When the ring detects an elevated stress level, your digital companion on Sight may gently suggest a breathing exercise or play calming music.

Note: Ring-Sight linking is for daily companionship and status awareness only, not for medical diagnosis or emergency assistance.

Helpful Tips
  • On first connection, confirm Bluetooth is enabled and allow the DeepSire app to use Bluetooth permissions.
  • Before measurement, ensure the ring is charged and properly positioned on your finger.
  • During single measurements, keep your hand still and avoid clenching or frequent finger movement.
  • Sleep data usually requires continuous nighttime wearing; sync time varies by app display.
  • If connection fails, try toggling Bluetooth off and on, or search for the device again in the app.
  • Regularly check for firmware updates in the app for a more stable experience.
  • All status data is for daily reference only — not intended for medical diagnosis or health conclusions.
  • When battery is below 5%, measurement is unavailable — charge promptly. The ring is not discoverable via Bluetooth while charging; disconnect the charger to pair.

Setup Complete

After completing these steps, you can view daily status data from your Touch Ring via the DeepSire app, or link the ring with your Sight device for a truly connected digital companionship experience.
