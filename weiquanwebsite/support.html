About Weiquan

Weiquan is an AI companionship and digital-life platform. You can create a digital companion with memory and personality, talk with it in the app, and continue that companionship on compatible devices such as a smart photo frame or wearable.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account or sign in?

Open the Weiquan app and follow the on-screen sign-in or registration steps. If you cannot receive a verification code, check your phone number or email, wait a few minutes, and try again. Still stuck? Email us with your registered phone or email address (do not send passwords).

How do I use a digital companion?

After signing in, create or choose a digital companion in the app. You can chat, leave memories, and continue the same companion on a compatible device that is linked to your account.

A compatible device will not connect

  • Confirm Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are turned on.
  • Keep the device nearby and powered on.
  • Restart the app and the device, then try pairing again from the in-app device page.

If it still fails, email us with the device name and a short description of the step where it stops.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can request account deletion from in-app settings, or email support@meeetta.com from the address or phone number linked to the account.
