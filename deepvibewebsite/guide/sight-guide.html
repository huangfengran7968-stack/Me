Sight Device

Quick start guide for your digital companionship journey

1

Power On & Connect

Connect your Sight device to power and wait for it to boot up. During the setup wizard, select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password.

Tip: Place your Sight device in an area with strong Wi-Fi signal for a stable connection.

2

Download the Deepvibe App

Download the Deepvibe app from the options below, or search "Deepvibe" in your app store to download and install the companion app.

Download on the App Store
Get it on Google Play
Direct APK Download

Supported Platforms: iOS 14.0+ / Android 10.0+

Tip: Before first use, ensure your phone has network access to download the app and complete the setup wizard.

3

Bind Your Device

Open the Deepvibe app, sign in or create an account, tap "Add Device", and scan the binding QR code on your Sight screen to complete pairing.

Device Bound Successfully
4

Create Your Digital Life

In the app, select "Create Digital Life", upload photos or video materials, and the system will automatically generate an interactive digital presence.

Best Results: Upload clear, front-facing photos with even lighting and a simple background.

5

Start Interacting

Once setup is complete, your digital life will appear on your Sight device. It automatically wakes when you approach — interact through touch or voice.

Helpful Tips
  • For first-time use, connect to a charger to ensure sufficient battery
  • Regularly update device firmware through the app for the latest features
  • Avoid placing the device in direct sunlight or high-temperature environments
  • Contact support via the app or visit our Help Center if you encounter any issues
